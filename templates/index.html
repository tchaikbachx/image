Instrument Inventory & Management System

Policies & Information

Welcome to the Tchaikbachx system. This portal allows students to browse available instruments and staff to manage checkouts and inventory details. Checking out an instrument is a privilege and responsibility. Our instrument collection is prioritized for Music Department activities. We have a limited number of instruments, and students will likely have to share for the semester. Following these guidelines helps ensure access to a working instrument.


Need help? Visit the Utility Hub on the right for service hours and contact details.

Music majors and Music Department student workers may also request to check out a variety of music technology equipment, including keyboards, microphones, a PA system, and more. Permissions may vary; contact Erik Jarvis for more information regarding these items.


Students enrolled in lessons or ensembles may request to borrow an instrument for the semester or academic year. Most instruments also come with a locker. Students who bring their own instrument may also request a locker. Checkout policies are listed below.


𝄞 WHO CAN CHECK OUT INSTRUMENTS?

  • Students enrolled in music department ensembles and/or lessons
  • Music majors (with permission from professor)
  • Students enrolled in a music department class (with permission from professor)
  • Staff and faculty enrolled in a music department ensemble

If you do not meet these requirements, you must have written permission from both a faculty member of the music department, and the Music Technical Assistant on staff

𝄞 HOW SHOULD I CARE FOR MY INSTRUMENT?

  • Never leave your instrument unattended
  • Never store your instrument in your car, an empty classroom, campus lounge, etc
  • Do not allow anyone else to play/borrow your instrument
  • Do not eat or drink (except water) near your instrument
  • Always place the instrument back in its case when not in use
  • Be sure your case is latched when picking it up or carrying
  • When storing your instrument, always use the proper orientation (e.g. for string instruments, never store the case strings-down)
  • Please review other instrument care practices with your instructor

𝄞 CHECK-OUT PROCEDURE

  • Fill out the Qualtrics form
  • Go to the instrument checkout room in Bucksbaum during open hours
  • Review these policies with a student worker
  • Confirm the check-in date (set a reminder in your calendar)
  • Enjoy a wonderful semester of music-making!

𝄞 CHECK-IN PROCEDURE

  • Bring your instrument to the instrument checkout room in Bucksbaum during open hours
  • Review the condition of the instrument with a student worker
  • If the inspection is fine, you’re done! If not, review the consequences and follow-up actions to be taken with a student worker and Music Technical Assistant on staff
  • For all late returns, a fine of $100/month will be applied. If returned within one week, the fine will be waived. If returned within the month, the fine will be reduced to $20.

NOTE: Checkout policies are subject to change at the discretion of the Music Department.
