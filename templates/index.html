Policies & Information

Welcome to the Tchaikbachx system. This portal allows students to browse available instruments and staff to manage checkouts and inventory details. Checking out an instrument is a privilege and responsibility. Our instrument collection is prioritized for Music Department activities. We have a limited number of instruments, and students will likely have to share for the semester. Following these guidelines helps ensure access to a working instrument. WHO CAN CHECK OUT INSTRUMENTS? • Students enrolled in Music Department ensembles • Students enrolled in Music Department lessons • Music Majors (with permission from professor) • Students enrolled in a Music Department class (with permission from professor) • Staff and faculty enrolled in a Music Department ensemble If you do not meet these requirements, you must have written permission from both a faculty member of the Music Department, and the Music Technical Assistant on staff. USE / CARE FOR INSTRUMENT • Never leave your instrument unattended. • More specifically: never store your instrument in your car, an empty classroom, campus lounge, etc. • Do not allow anyone else to play / borrow your instrument. • Do not eat or drink (except water) near your instrument. • Always place the instrument back in its case when not in use. • Be sure your case is latched when picking it up or carrying. • When storing your instrument, always use the proper orientation o Eg: For string instruments, never store the case strings-down • Please review other instrument care practices with your instructor. CHECK-OUT PROCEDURE • Fill out the Qualtrics form • Go to the Instrument Checkout Room in Bucksbaum during open hours • Review these policies with a student worker • Confirm the check-in date (set a reminder in your calendar) • Enjoy a wonderful semester of music-making CHECK-IN PROCEDURE • Bring your instrument to the Instrument Checkout room in Bucksbaum during open hours • Review the condition of the instrument with a student worker • If the inspection is fine, you’re done! If not, review the consequences and follow-up actions to be taken with a student worker and Music Technical Assistant on staff • For all late returns, a fine of $100/month will be applied. If returned within one week, the fine will be waived. If returned within the month, the fine will be reduced to $20. Checkout policies are subject to change at the discretion of the Music Department.

End-of-semester Notice: Please ensure all instruments are returned or renewed by the end of finals week.
